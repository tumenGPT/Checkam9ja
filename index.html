How Checkam9ja thinks

A registration number alone cannot prove the physical item is genuine. A copied genuine number can appear on a counterfeit package. Checkam9ja is designed to compare the registration record with the product name, applicant/manufacturer and other package details.

Prototype • Not affiliated with or endorsed by NAFDAC. A “verified record” means registration details were found/matched; it is not a guarantee that the physical item is genuine.
